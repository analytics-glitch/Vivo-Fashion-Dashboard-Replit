Metric definitions
% Consumption / % Available — each group's share of total net usage vs. total available stock over the window.
Gap (pp) = % Available − % Consumption. + overstock holds more than it uses · − short uses faster than its stock share · ± balanced within ±3pp.
Weekly covers = Available ÷ (Consumption ÷ weeks). Below 1 = under a week of cover.
Verdict — Buy short (shortfall < 0, cover < ~4wk, or Gap ≤ −3pp) · Clear overstock (Gap ≥ +3pp or > ~52wk cover) · Idle stock but ~no usage in the window · Hold balanced.
Tied-up KES — capital locked in the group's available stock.
