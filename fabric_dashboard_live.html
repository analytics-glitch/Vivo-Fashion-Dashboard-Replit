Fabrics blocking "Avg metres / garment"

The Avg metres / garment KPI drops any Done-DPS manufacturing order whose fabric has no kg→metre conversion in Odoo, so the average is understated. These are the fabrics responsible — fix each one's kg-per-metre in Odoo to grow KPI coverage. A garment can be blocked by more than one fabric, so the counts may sum to more than the total excluded.

Unmatched purchase lines

Loading…

These are received purchase-order lines whose product is not counted in the supplier totals or cost-per-metre, because it has no row in the fabric master or is not categorized as Fabric in Odoo. Their received quantity and value are silently dropped and never trigger the missing kg-per-metre warning. Fix each one by correcting the product's category / master record in Odoo; its value then flows into the supplier figures automatically. Not in fabric master no master row · Not categorized as Fabric e.g. tagged Trim.
