---
name: Style Development operating model
description: Durable readiness, timing, exit, and capacity rules for the Product Development Tracker.
---

Sample fabric selection must use live Fabric BI products. Availability is canonical available stock converted to metres; indicative COGS uses live cost and category-level metres per garment. Adoption readiness is a soft gate: missing stock, season, COGS, price, or a COGS result above 32% of VAT-exclusive selling price requires a recorded override rather than blocking progress absolutely.

**Why:** Product-development decisions need current material feasibility without letting incomplete source data halt urgent work invisibly.

**How to apply:** Keep fabric choice constrained to live products, show the chosen colour plus sibling colours, and preserve structured missing/warning reasons in the API and UI.

Lifecycle reporting uses working days and reports queue, work, and total intervals separately using median, P80, observation count, and standards. Parallel CAD grading and set-sample ordering remain separate branches that converge before production.

**Why:** Calendar-day averages hide weekends, queues, and the actual source of delay.

**How to apply:** Do not collapse queue and work time or replace median/P80 with averages. The happy path is 13 working days while the business target remains 4–5 weeks.

Pattern assignments reference canonical workspace-team members by stable ID; display names always come from Settings. CAD and Ken Knit are ongoing non-person pattern routes and remain selectable destinations; their workloads are tracked separately from individual capacity. CAD is being phased out gradually rather than immediately, and the unaffected CAD pipeline stage still covers grading, marker making, and set-sample processing. Never store new person assignments as labels or let the tracker create phantom people. Pattern capacity is derived from configured effective capacity (the core team currently totals 3.5), reduced by dated unavailability, at two working days per pattern and five working days per week. Abigail, Re, and Tony may hold assigned work but contribute no capacity unless the business explicitly configures it. The actionable personal queue includes Pattern and Transfer to CAD, uses an optional per-style effort override, and compares people by effort-weeks rather than total holdings. Reassignment reasons are configurable but must remain classified as operational or style-related. Only style-related moves flag a style as having been passed around; operational batches preserve immutable before/after display-name snapshots for both total assignment load and the actionable queue. On-hold and cancelled exits require one exact reason, and the reached stage is frozen at exit time.

**Why:** Name-based assignments split one person after a rename and let misspellings create fake team members. CAD staff can continue finishing pattern work during the gradual phase-out, but team routes must not inflate individual capacity or weeks-of-work figures. Total style counts and routed work distort the actual pattern queue, especially for the half-capacity team lead. Absence and workload balancing should explain forecast capacity without implying a style problem, while specialist or fresh-eye handoffs are genuine style signals. Immutable snapshots keep later Settings-name changes from rewriting what a redistribution did.

**How to apply:** Compare and group current assignments by person/route key, then join the current Settings name for display. Keep CAD and Ken Knit in every valid destination selector, but exclude both team routes from personal capacity and individual queue-week calculations. Show CAD's active count and styles in a dedicated Capacity section, separate from the 3.5 internal makers, non-capacity people, Ken Knit, and unassigned work; preview every capacity-bearing person's before/after load and effort-weeks before bulk reassigning CAD work. Recalculate load from active tracker records, subtract overlapping unavailable capacity for the planning date, and show queue stage, oldest wait, effort, and category mix. Rebalance suggestions may propose specific styles only when they reduce the effort-week spread; they remain review-only and use the normal audited reassignment flow. Require a classified reason for every changed assignment, and store the reason label/category plus optional note with the audit event. Keep exit reasons limited to pattern will not work, wrong for the season, no fabric in stock, and margin too high.
