---
name: Stock to sales planning evidence
description: How monthly stock to sales reports relate to Range Plans and live BI metrics.
---

Treat each monthly Stock to Sales report as frozen planning evidence: record the covered month, pull date, external file link, and the monthly Range Plan it informed. Keep the archive newest-first and clearly label it as a point-in-time snapshot rather than a live view.

**Why:** The full spreadsheet is useful for detailed slicing, but rebuilding or silently refreshing it would erase the exact information available when a planning decision was made. The plan itself should retain only the decision figures needed for everyday use.

**How to apply:** Attach at most one archived snapshot to a monthly Range Plan. Keep live summary fields such as units sold, weeks of cover, and sell-through in the plan when the BI source is trustworthy; link out to the frozen report for deeper analysis.
