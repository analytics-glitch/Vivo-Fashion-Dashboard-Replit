description: Confirmed visual and hierarchy rules for Atelier and future Customer Experience Workspace surfaces.

Atelier has its own identity rather than inheriting Product Workspace navy/gold: clay is the sole brand accent, supported by ink, paper, and warm neutral surfaces. Use Playfair Display for headings and DM Sans for operational text.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that Atelier and the future Customer Experience Workspace should feel distinct from Product Workspace while retaining its typography. Mixing green, orange, and status colors as decorative accents made the operational hierarchy unclear.

**How to apply:** Reserve amber for attention, sage for completion, and brick for overdue/error states. Use schema-aligned Title Case status labels everywhere. Remove aggregate BI filters and technical health/status chrome from single-record operational screens, and make the record's primary action visually stronger than read-only reference panels.
