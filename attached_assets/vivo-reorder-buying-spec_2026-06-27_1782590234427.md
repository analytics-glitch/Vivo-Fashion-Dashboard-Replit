# Vivo Fashion Group — Re-Order / Buying Redesign
### Implementation specification — best buying decisions for SOR + a shorter cash-conversion cycle

Prepared 27 June 2026 · front-end audit + 5-expert council (ratified 5/5) · Page: `/re-order`
Companion file: `vivo-reorder-buying-wireframe_2026-06-27.png`.

---

## 1. Objective

The best buying decision is the one that **buys the fewest units that capture the most demand, arriving in time, weighted by margin, net of pipeline and net of stock you already hold dead — within a budget funded by clearing the slow tail.** That serves both goals:

- **SOR** = units_sold ÷ (units_sold + stock): buy the right depth of proven winners so they don't stock out (numerator up) and don't over-cut slow styles (denominator down).
- **CCC** (cash-conversion cycle): buying is where cash is committed, so the buy list is the primary CCC control. Over-buying or buying slow lengthens DIO; clearing dead stock to fund the buy shortens it.

---

## 2. Council verdict

A five-person panel (buyer/range-planner, demand-forecasting/data-science, supply-chain & working-capital, garment production planner, product/UX) **unanimously ratified** the redesign (5/5), each with one binding amendment, all folded in:

| Discipline | Binding amendment |
|---|---|
| Buyer / range-planner | **Open-To-Buy is the hard frame** — rank candidates and cut the list at the budget line; one queue, two labelled buckets (proven core/replen ~70%, newness bets ~30%); replace raw-SOR rank with margin × demand + a hard unit floor. |
| Forecasting / data-science | **Make the buy a distribution, not a number** — Bayesian shrinkage for small n, censored-demand correction, and a critical-fractile quantity that balances lost-margin (SOR) vs holding/cash (CCC). |
| Supply-chain / working-capital | **Fund the OTB purse from clearance first** — liquidate the 60d+ tail, ring-fence that cash; rank in payback-days/GMROI and cap in KES; reject MOQ economics that don't pay. |
| Garment production | **Net against WIP and fabric before ranking** — never re-cut what's on the line or with no fabric; expose make-feasibility; consolidate cuts on shared fabric/blocks. |
| Product / UX | **The unit of the screen is a Buy Decision Card** (qty + confidence band + GMROI + make-feasibility + aged-stock + OTB meter) with one Approve/Adjust/Decline that writes the PO; merge the three tables into one decision view. |

## 4. The buying-decision model

Per candidate style (produce/buy decision = style total):

```
recommended_buy = Q*( demand over (lead_time + target_cover) )
                  − on_hand_stores − warehouse − open_POs − WIP        # net inventory position
                  , rounded to cut-min (MOQ) and the size curve,
                  only if units_sold ≥ confidence floor AND no sellable aged stock of the style
```

**Demand** is a posterior rate, not raw history:
- **Bayesian shrinkage** (Gamma-Poisson / negative-binomial) with the **category/peer rate as the prior**: a style that sold 2 units in 1 week shrinks hard toward the category rate (→ no buy); the 679-unit winner barely moves. Use **TSB/Croston** for lumpy series; report a **predictive interval (p10/p50/p90)**, never a bare point.
- **Censored-demand correction**: treat stock-out days as right-censored — fit the rate on **in-stock days only** (units ÷ in-stock-days). This reverses the under-buy of winners.
- **Trend & seasonality** modulate the rate (accelerating ↑, decelerating ↓; seasonal styles use a seasonal profile).
- **Size curve** estimated hierarchically (Dirichlet-multinomial pooled by category/fit), so sparse styles borrow strength; buy to the curve, not flat.

**Quantity encodes uncertainty** via the **critical fractile** `Q* = Cu ÷ (Cu + Co)`:
- `Cu` = under-stock cost = lost gross margin → protects **SOR** (high-margin proven winners buy near p90).
- `Co` = over-stock cost = holding + expected markdown + cash cost → protects **CCC** (noisy/thin styles buy near p50 or skip).

**Ranking & gating:** rank by **opportunity value (expected margin × demand) / payback-days / GMROI** — never by SOR%. Hard gates before a style is eligible: a **minimum-units confidence floor**, **no sellable aged stock** of the style elsewhere, and **make-feasibility** (§6).

## 5. Open-To-Buy — the hard frame, funded by clearance

- **OTB is a constraint the list is solved against, not a meter.** Rank candidates by payback-days/GMROI and **cut the list at the committed-KES budget line**: "these N styles fit your OTB; these M are above the line, deprioritised." A buy list that can recommend more cash than you have launders over-commitment.
- **Fund the purse from clearance first.** No incremental cut is approved while sellable 60d+ stock sits trapped: liquidate the tail (markdown / IBT to a proven seller / return-to-warehouse) → the cash released **is** the OTB budget. This is the single biggest CCC win and it makes the buy self-funding.
- **MOQ economics gate.** Reject any cut where `cut-min × unit cost` exceeds projected gross margin over the lead-time + cover horizon — rounding a thin-demand style up to its minimum cut is how you manufacture the next aged tail.
- **Express everything in cash:** per row and in total — units → KES committed (cut-min × cost) → projected DIO add → payback-days → GMROI, with a running cumulative-KES-vs-OTB column; footer shows KES committed, KES released from clearance, net cash movement, and projected group DIO/CCC delta.

---

## 6. Make-feasibility (this is a production cut, not a supplier PO)

Three hard gates before a candidate can rank, netted against the Production Tracker and fabric:

- **WIP netting** — subtract open production orders; never re-cut a style already on the line.
- **Fabric gate** — no fabric on hand or on order → no cut; offer a **substitute** on the same fabric platform/block instead.
- **Cut-min & capacity** — round to the minimum economical cut (marker/lay efficiency) and check it fits available factory capacity this period.

Each card shows the make-feasibility verdict: **FEASIBLE re-cut** (make lead-time < cover-left) / **CLEARANCE** (un-recoverable in time) / **SUBSTITUTE** (no fabric). Highest-leverage production move: **fabric-platform / shared-block consolidation** — batch styles sharing fabric and blocks into one cut, which lowers the effective cut-min, improves fabric yield and shortens lead time (helps SOR and CCC at once).

---

## 7. Decision separation & coordination

- **Produce/buy** (style total) lives here and writes a production cut / PO. **Allocate-to-store** is the Replenishment and IBT engines (already specced). One number, deduped — not two conflicting buys.
- **One OTB queue, two labelled buckets:** proven core/replenishment (~70%) and newness bets (~30%), so velocity-ranking doesn't starve newness and newness punts don't crowd out bankable re-orders.
- **Newness rule:** the 90-day window *flags* newness but doesn't by itself confer buy priority — a 95-day-old style selling 679 units outranks an 8-day-old style selling 2. (The 21-day transfer guard governs IBT, not buying.)

## 8. Metrics

Replace the bare SOR% sort and the mystery "Replenishment accuracy 14.8%" tile:

- **Define "replenishment/buy accuracy"** = actual vs recommended (units and direction), with a target band, and **feed it back** to calibrate the forecast (a low score should auto-flag over/under-sizing, not sit unexplained).
- KPI tiles: **OTB remaining (KES)** · **recommended buy value (KES, pending)** · **at-risk SOR (KES of lost sell-through if no action)** · **projected blended GMROI** · **cash released from clearance**. Add **decisions cleared / avg time-to-decision** (speed = cash cycle).
- Per candidate: expected margin, GMROI, payback-days, projected SOR and DIO/CCC impact.

---

## 9. Live, event-driven data

Same platform principle as Replenishment/IBT: the buy list must reflect current state. A **sale**, **stock movement**, **receipt**, **production update (WIP)**, **fabric movement**, **price/markdown change** or **new-style first sale** updates the live state and re-evaluates the affected candidates — so a style's confidence, net position and feasibility move in real time (e.g. the 1-unit style stops being "critical" as more data arrives; a winner's buy grows as censored-corrected demand updates). State near-real-time (Odoo webhooks/CDC on POS sales, stock moves, pickings, MO/WIP, fabric, price); scoring stats recompute on a short cadence; reconcile against Odoo nightly. Keep an immutable snapshot of each recommendation (for the accuracy feedback loop) separate from the live state.

## 10. UI / UX

**Fix first:** the raw-SOR% ranking that puts 1–2-unit styles on top — gate on volume/confidence and rank by opportunity value.

**The unit of the screen is a Buy Decision Card per style**, not a table row. Each card carries: recommended buy qty with **p10/p50/p90 band**, confidence/volume flag, expected margin + GMROI + payback-days, **make-feasibility light** (fabric / lead-time / capacity → FEASIBLE / CLEARANCE / SUBSTITUTE), **aged-stock-elsewhere** callout, KES committed, and the running OTB meter. One control: **Approve / Adjust / Decline** — Approve writes the cut/PO; Adjust opens a qty stepper. The Replenishment-by-Colour and Aged-Stock tables become **inputs/badges/drawers on the card**, not separate destinations.

**Layout, top-to-bottom:** (1) sticky header — OTB meter + at-risk-SOR + recommended-buy-value tiles + "as of today · 28d lookback"; (2) sort/filter bar defaulting to "rank by opportunity value", with the core/newness bucket toggle; (3) vertical stack of Buy Decision Cards, urgent first, with the OTB budget line drawn across (above-budget candidates greyed below it); (4) card drawer → size-curve detail, aged-stock-elsewhere, substitute options; (5) clearance-funding panel (tail to liquidate → cash released → OTB purse); (6) running footer — decisions, KES committed, KES released, OTB left, projected CCC delta.

**Date:** operational as-of-today (no reporting date range); one labelled demand-lookback (default 28 days). Don't mutate the global filter.

## 13. Open decisions (the knobs)

1. Confidence/volume floor (minimum lifetime units before a style is buy-eligible).
2. `Cu` / `Co` cost parameters (lost-margin vs holding+markdown+cash) that set the critical fractile.
3. Target cover weeks (by class) and the safety-stock service level.
4. OTB budget per period and the core-vs-newness split.
5. Cut-min (MOQ) and capacity source; fabric-platform/block groupings.
6. Aged-stock threshold (days) and the clearance/markdown rule that funds the purse.

---

## Expected impact

This is the highest-leverage page for both objectives because it controls where cash enters. The near-term win is **cash-conversion**: stop re-cutting noise and styles already held dead, fund the buy by clearing the 60d+ tail, and cap commitments to an OTB purse — directly shortening DIO/CCC in a network already overstocked (~30% sell-through, 10–15 weeks cover). The **SOR** win comes from correctly sizing the *real* winners (censored-corrected, confidence-banded, size-curve-aware) so they don't stock out, while the confidence floor stops 1–2-unit noise from consuming cash. Validate with a backtest once style-level sales + stock history, open-PO/WIP, fabric and cost/margin are available.
