# Vivo Junction Atelier Workspace — Blueprint

Full page (audit findings, schema, KPIs, styled): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/a699aeb7-b895-4ca4-b9b7-a02384660d8e

Source data: `2026 Vivo Junction Instore Tailoring & Adjustments Log 1.xlsx` (82 job rows, 10 Jun–22 Aug 2026; Requisitions tab unused).

## Scope decisions made in discussion (2026-08-28)

- **Architecture**: build as a new module *inside* the existing Vivo Replit project (which already hosts the customer database, BI dashboard, and customer community app/Style DNA) — not a separate connected app. Customer creation goes through a controlled service layer, never raw writes from atelier screens, to protect the shared customer table from atelier-level bugs. Always search by normalized phone before creating a customer, to avoid duplicates in the master record.
- **Charging**: service is free today, may become chargeable later. One `charge_amount` field on `jobs`, default 0, covers this — no separate is_chargeable flag needed.
- **Customer linking**: tightly coupled — jobs write into the *actual* shared customers table (same project/database), not a copy. New customers created at the atelier counter become real, project-wide customer records.
- **Measurements**: tailors record customer measurements and fit preferences; these live on the **shared customer profile** (new `customer_measurements` table, versioned/history, not overwritten) — visible beyond just the atelier.
- **Photo capture**: yes — a quick garment-condition photo at intake (`intake_photo_url`, `condition_notes` on `jobs`), for damage-dispute protection.
- **Staff visibility**: all tailors see all jobs (a customer may return when a different tailor is on shift) — no per-tailor restriction. Admin-only restriction is for staff management, alteration-type/branch config, and full financial reporting.
- **Tailor entry**: dropdown of existing staff with an inline "add new" option — no separate admin step needed to log a job with a new tailor.
- **UI tone**: deliberately plain — short labels, minimal steps, no jargon, usable by any staff member regardless of tech background.
- **Style DNA**: lives in the customer community app, understood to sit centrally in the data warehouse and be accessible from multiple front-ends. URL to be shared and reviewed before deciding exact integration (link-out vs. pulling specific fields) — placeholder + TODO in the prompt for now.
- **Community app membership**: not yet decided as mandatory vs. optional at atelier intake. Recommended default: optional (offered as the natural way to save details, doesn't block service if declined) — flagged as an open question in the prompt, pending review of the community app URL.
- **Terms & Conditions / SLA**: user will provide a draft policy for Claude to help refine; a `policies` table is scaffolded in the schema but the legal/policy content itself is intentionally left for the user's draft, not invented.
- **Materials/Requisitions tracking**: left out of the build for now (old sheet's tab was unused for ~2.5 months) — flagged as a possible future addition, not decided either way yet.
