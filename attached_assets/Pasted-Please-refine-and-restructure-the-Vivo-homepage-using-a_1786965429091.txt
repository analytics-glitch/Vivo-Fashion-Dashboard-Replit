Please refine and restructure the Vivo homepage using a community-first approach.

IMPORTANT:

* The primary purpose of the app is to build and engage the Vivo community.
* Online shopping and loyalty are secondary supporting features.
* Do not redesign the homepage as a conventional online store.
* Preserve all existing functionality, routes, authentication, database connections, content and business logic.
* Do not delete existing features; move them to more appropriate pages where necessary.

The homepage should feel like a curated weekly overview of the Vivo community rather than displaying the entire community feed.

REVISED HOMEPAGE ORDER

1. HERO

Keep a strong Vivo campaign or community hero image.

The hero can introduce:

* A current campaign
* A community theme
* A seasonal conversation
* A featured Vivo story

Include one relevant call to action such as:

* Join the Conversation
* Explore the Story
* Participate Now
* Discover More

Shopping does not need to be the primary hero action every week.

2. THIS WEEK’S MISSION

Move the current weekly mission close to the top of the homepage.

Show:

* Mission title
* Short description
* Participation reward, where applicable
* Number of members who have participated
* “Enter Now” button

Keep the full submission flow inside the Community page.

3. COMMUNITY FEED PREVIEW

Show a maximum of three or four featured community posts.

Prioritise:

* Member outfit posts
* Personal stories
* Style conversations
* Strong visual posts
* Posts receiving meaningful community engagement

Add one clear “View Community” button leading to the complete Community feed.

Do not show the entire continuously scrolling community feed on the homepage.

4. FRESH FROM VIVO

Keep the short-form video and reels carousel.

Show a maximum of five videos at a time and include a “View All” link.

Videos may include:

* Styling content
* Behind the scenes
* Member stories
* Vivo campaigns
* Creator features
* Event highlights

5. STYLE QUESTION OF THE WEEK

Show one featured style question or poll.

Allow members to answer directly or open the complete conversation in Community.

Do not display several separate text-question cards on the homepage.

6. UPCOMING EVENT

Show only the next relevant Vivo event.

Include:

* Event name
* Date
* Time
* Location or online status
* RSVP button

Keep the complete events calendar on the Community page.

7. COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHT

Combine “This Week’s Jewel” and “Community Voices” into one section called “Community Spotlight.”

Feature one member each week with:

* Member name
* Image
* Short quote or story excerpt
* Community tier or badge, where relevant
* “Read Her Story” button

Avoid showing several separate quote sections.

8. STYLED FOR YOU

Keep Styled for You as a supporting personalised experience.

Combine it with the existing “Help us dress you better” survey.

For members who have not completed their preferences, show:

* “Styled for You”
* A short explanation
* “Personalise My Style” button
* Optional points incentive
* “Maybe Later” link

For members who have opted in, show weekly personalised outfits or content recommendations.

Styled for You may recommend:

* Community posts
* Styling conversations
* Events
* Vivo products
* Complete outfits

It should not recommend products only.

9. SHOP COMMUNITY LOOKS

Add a compact shoppable section featuring outfits worn by community members, creators or Vivo stylists.

Each look may include:

* Member or curator name
* Outfit image
* Short styling note
* Tagged Vivo products
* “Shop the Look” button

Keep this section community-led and secondary to engagement content.

10. MEMBER REWARDS

Keep one compact loyalty preview showing:

* Available points
* Current tier
* Progress towards the next tier
* “View Rewards” button

The detailed loyalty experience should remain on the Rewards page.

11. VIVO STORIES

Show no more than three editorial stories.

Stories may include:

* Community profiles
* Styling stories
* Vivo milestones
* Campaign features
* Store or event news

Add a “View All Stories” link.

12. GIVE YOUR VIVO A SECOND LIFE

Keep the sustainability initiative as a small closing homepage feature or move it into Account under Sustainability if the homepage remains too long.

CONTENT TO MOVE

Move the following to the full Community page:

* Complete member feed
* Additional member posts
* Additional style questions
* Full events calendar
* Boards We Love
* All reels and videos
* Mission submissions
* Longer community conversations

Move Community Fit Notes to the relevant product pages.

Move See It on You to Shop and individual product pages.

Move detailed company and store news to Vivo Stories.

DESIGN REFINEMENTS

* Retain the premium editorial Vivo identity
* Reduce repeated white bordered cards
* Use larger community photography
* Fix all missing or broken images
* Increase text readability and navigation size
* Use consistent image ratios
* Reduce repetitive orange labels
* Create stronger spacing between major sections
* Make the homepage wider and better balanced on desktop
* Maintain a polished mobile-first design
* Clearly separate community content from supporting shopping and rewards content

The final homepage should feel like the curated front door to the Vivo community: lively, welcoming, inspirational and easy to participate in, with shopping and loyalty integrated naturally rather than dominating the experience.
