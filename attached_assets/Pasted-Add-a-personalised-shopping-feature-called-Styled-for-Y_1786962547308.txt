Add a personalised shopping feature called “Styled for You.”

The feature should appear in three connected areas:

1. HOME PAGE

Add a “Styled for You” section after the main hero campaign and before “New This Week.”

For customers who have opted in:

* Display a horizontally scrollable carousel of weekly personalised product and outfit recommendations
* Include the heading “Styled for You”
* Include the supporting text “Your weekly picks are here.”
* Add a “View All” link
* Show a small “Updated weekly” label

For customers who have not opted in:

* Display an elegant invitation card using a Vivo lifestyle image
* Heading: “Styled for You”
* Supporting text: “Get weekly outfit and product recommendations selected around your style, size and preferences.”
* Primary button: “Personalise My Style”
* Secondary link: “Maybe Later”

Do not automatically enrol customers. Clearly request their consent before activating weekly recommendations.

2. SHOP PAGE

Add “Styled for You” as a dedicated shopping tab or collection.

This page should show all personalised recommendations in a two-column product grid. Include optional sections such as:

* Complete the Look
* Workwear Picks
* New This Week
* Based on Your Favourites
* Recommended in Your Size

Allow customers to refresh their preferences from this page.

3. ACCOUNT PAGE

Add a “Style Preferences” section where customers can opt in or out and update:

* Clothing size
* Preferred fit
* Favourite colours
* Preferred categories
* Workwear, casual, occasionwear or activewear interests
* Styles they would prefer not to see
* Recommendation frequency
* Push-notification and email preferences

Add a visible toggle labelled “Weekly Styled for You recommendations.”

Explain that customers can change their preferences or opt out at any time.

Use the customer’s existing browsing, wishlist and purchase data only where permission has been provided. Provide useful general recommendations when insufficient customer data is available.

Keep the feature visually premium, simple and consistent with the rest of the Vivo app. Preserve all existing functionality and database connections.
