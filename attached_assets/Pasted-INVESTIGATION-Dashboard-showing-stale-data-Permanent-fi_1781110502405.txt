Test by killing api_pg.py and sync_incremental.py manually and confirming they restart automatically within 10 seconds

Build a self-healing monitoring system:

If either check fails, automatically restart the failed process

Add a /api/sync-status endpoint to api_pg.py that returns: last sync time, minutes since last sync, sync health (OK/WARNING/CRITICAL), and which stores were last synced
