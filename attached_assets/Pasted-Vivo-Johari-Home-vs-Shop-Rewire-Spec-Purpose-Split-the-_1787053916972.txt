Vivo Johari — Home vs Shop Rewire Spec
Purpose: Split the current Home page so it becomes purely community/editorial/brand content, with every shopping entry point (buy CTAs, "Shop the Look" links, product tag pills, product recommendation modules) relocated to the Shop section. Paste this into Replit's agent as the implementation brief.

Rule of thumb: If a component's job is to get someone to look at or buy a product, it belongs in Shop. If its job is to get someone to read, react, post, or feel part of the community, it stays on Home.

Implementation notes for the Replit agent
Treat this as moving components, not just hiding them — VivoEdits should become a child of the Community route/page, not conditionally rendered on Home.
StyledForYou splits into two components: a lightweight StyledForYouOptIn (single toggle/CTA, no questions) that stays on Home, and the full quiz/recommendation-rail logic that moves to Shop. Don't just hide the quiz with CSS — it should live in the Shop bundle so Home isn't loading unused quiz logic or state.
The product-tag pills on community post cards ("Looks & conversations we loved," "Style Question of the Week") are likely a shared <ProductTag> component — on Home, render the post card without it (or pass a showProductTags={false} prop); keep the component available for use inside Shop/product pages.
On the "Vivo Edits" cards once they live in Community, remove the <ShopTheLookButton> (or equivalent) from the "Worn by the Community" sub-cards; leave "Explore Her Style" only if it routes to a lookbook/story view, not checkout.
Any API/data-fetching tied to the moved quiz/recommendation logic should move with it to the Shop route so Home's initial load isn't fetching shopping data it no longer displays.
Keep the global nav (points, wishlist, cart icon) untouched — those are app-wide, not Home-specific.
After the move, do a pass on Home for any remaining "Shop," "Buy," "Browse," or price-mention copy that slipped through.


Mobile-specific fixes
Top nav placement. On mobile the primary nav (Home / Shop / Community / Rewards / Account / Help, points badge, wishlist, bag, avatar) currently renders as a bottom bar — move it to a fixed/sticky header at the top of the viewport, matching desktop. If a bottom bar pattern is being used for thumb-reachability, that's a separate design decision to confirm with the user before reintroducing it — for now, top nav on mobile, same as desktop.
Use position: sticky; top: 0; (or fixed, with matching content padding-top to avoid overlap) rather than a bottom tab bar component.
Make sure the nav collapses sensibly (e.g. hamburger/overflow menu for the six items + icons) rather than compressing into unreadable tap targets.
Image resizing. Several images (hero banner, community post photos, reel thumbnails, Vivo Edits cards, story cards) are laid out at desktop aspect ratios/widths and need mobile-specific sizing:
Serve responsive images (srcset/sizes or the framework's image component) so mobile doesn't download desktop-resolution assets.
Recheck aspect ratios at mobile breakpoints — the two-column grids (community posts, Vivo Edits, story cards) should likely collapse to a single column on small screens rather than shrinking two columns down to illegibly small cards.
Verify the hero image crops sensibly on mobile (the current portrait photo may need a mobile-specific crop/focal point rather than just scaling down the desktop crop).
