IMPORTANT:

* Preserve all existing functionality, routes, authentication, database connections and business logic.
* This is a visual redesign, not a rebuild.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Make the Vivo app feel:

* Premium and modern
* Feminine, warm and inclusive
* Editorial and image-led
* Clean and easy to navigate
* Representative of stylish African women
* Like a seamless combination of fashion shopping, community and loyalty rewards

Use Vivo’s official brand colours and typography where provided. Use generous white space, clean line icons, consistent spacing and subtle transitions. Avoid excessive rounded cards, heavy shadows, gradients, borders or a generic dashboard appearance.
