Data model
Source: customer transaction data (Odoo POS/ERP)

For the recency calculation, you need:

SELECT
  customer_id,
  store_name,
  MAX(order_date) AS last_purchase_date,
  DATEDIFF(CURDATE(), MAX(order_date)) AS days_since_last_purchase,
  COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) AS total_orders,
  SUM(amount_total) / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) AS avg_spend_per_visit
FROM pos_orders
WHERE customer_id IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY customer_id, store_name

Then assign each (customer_id, store_name) pair to a bucket based on days_since_last_purchase.

Important: A customer who shops at multiple stores should appear in the recency bucket corresponding to their MOST RECENT purchase across ALL selected stores, not once per store. However, in the per-store breakdown inside each expanded card, attribute that customer to the store where their most recent purchase occurred.

Source: footfall data
