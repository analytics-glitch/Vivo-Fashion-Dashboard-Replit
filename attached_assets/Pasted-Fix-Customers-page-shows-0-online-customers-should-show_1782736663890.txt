Fix: Customers page shows 0 online customers (should show online customer counts and attribute online orders correctly)
The Customers page reports 0 online customers and treats online orders as walk-in. The underlying data is now correct — online sales in all_sales carry real customer_ids (Shop Zetu was just backfilled via GraphQL enrichment; vivowoman already had them). This is now a page/query bug, not a data gap.
How online vs retail is identified in the data:
Online sales in all_sales are identified by country = 'Online' (equivalently pos_location_name starting with 'Online'), across these store_ids:

shop-zetu — Shop Zetu online store (now has customer_id, ~99.97% coverage)
vivowoman — Shopify women's store (online)
plus small vivo-uganda / vivo-rwanda online volumes

Retail/POS is everything else (Odoo stores: vivofashiongroup, vivo-rwanda, vivo-uganda physical locations).
What's wrong now:
The customer endpoints (/api/customers, /api/customers/walk-ins, /api/customer-type-spend, and related) build a channel_filter on pos_location_name IN (...) from a channel parameter the frontend passes. The frontend is passing a channel label that does not exactly match the pos_location_name strings in the data, so the online filter matches nothing and online customers fall through to walk-in / zero.
The actual pos_location_name values for online are:

Online - Shop Zetu
Online - vivowoman
Online - vivo-uganda
Online Orders Location (legacy, small)

What to change:

Channel/online filtering — wherever the page or its endpoints split online vs retail, base "online" on all_sales.country = 'Online' (the reliable flag) rather than an exact pos_location_name string match. If the frontend sends a channel selector ("Online" / "Retail"), map "Online" → country = 'Online' and "Retail" → country <> 'Online' (or country IN ('Kenya','Uganda','Rwanda')), instead of passing a pos_location_name literal that has to match exactly.
Online customer count — online identified customers = COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) from all_sales where country='Online' and customer_id IS NOT NULL. This should now be in the thousands (Shop Zetu ~149K distinct across history, plus vivowoman), not 0.
Guest vs walk-in — online orders with customer_id IS NULL are guest checkouts, not walk-ins. Keep them in a "Guest" bucket for online, distinct from in-store "Walk-in" (retail anonymous sales). Don't lump online guests into walk-in.
New/Returning split — the existing logic uses first-ever purchase date over customer_type IN ('new','returning','registered'). Online rows carry customer_type of 'New'/'Returning' (capitalized; the queries already LOWER() them, so they qualify). Confirm online customers now flow into the New/Returning segmentation once the channel filter is fixed.

Verify after the change:

Customers page with no channel filter: online customers appear in the totals (thousands, not 0).
Filtering channel = Online: shows online identified customers + a separate Guest count, with online orders attributed to their customer_id where present.
Retail filter unchanged (POS registered/walk-in behavior preserved).
Financial totals unchanged — this is a customer-counting fix only, no change to sales/revenue.

Don't modify the sync pipeline or all_sales data — the customer_ids are already correctly populated. This is query/display logic on the Customers page and its API endpoints only.
